One Solutions Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

One Solutions provides OneEngine and related business software and integrations. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when customers and authorised users use our services.

Information we process

Depending on the services enabled, we may process account and business information, customer contact details, transaction and appointment information, device and security information, and communications submitted through connected services such as WhatsApp.

How information is used

We process information to provide and secure the service, authenticate users, deliver requested communications, operate customer-support and business workflows, maintain records, diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

WhatsApp and connected services

When a customer enables a third-party connector, OneEngine processes information required to provide that integration. WhatsApp messages and related identifiers may be processed to deliver messages, receive replies, associate conversations with authorised business records, and execute configured workflows. Third-party services are also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers and connected platforms only as necessary to operate requested functionality, maintain infrastructure and security, or meet legal requirements.

Security and retention

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service. Information is retained only as required for service operation, contractual requirements, security, record keeping, and applicable law.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding their personal information. Requests are subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
